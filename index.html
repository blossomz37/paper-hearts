Welcome to Paper Hearts

A little chance.
A lot of chemistry.

Story spark gives you a complication and a question. Playful fortune gives you a fictional romantic outcome.

  1. Choose a romance universe, relationship, and heat.
  2. Pick a setting, a character number, and a trope number—or choose Surprise me.
  3. Keep the result, change its relationship or heat, or fold again.

Matchmaker: pair a character card and a trope card with the same clue word. Any found pair can become a new result. Practice reveals the deck; there is no timer. Play alone, or invite one of eight rivals to sit across the table: you take turns, a match earns another go, and whoever finds more pairs wins. Each rival remembers the board differently.

Saved results stay in this browser where storage is available. Downloads and result codes travel with you. A local preview link only works on your computer; send the HTML file and a result code to someone else.

Handwritten combinations, not live AI. All characters are adults. Sensual means non-graphic; some worlds include danger or suspense. Paper sounds can be switched off under Customize.
